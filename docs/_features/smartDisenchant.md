A Smart Disenchant button next to your dust on the Crafting page disenchants cards in bulk. It opens a window listing every card you can disenchant, with a Normal column and a Shiny column. Each column is split by rarity, and each card you own more than one of is split by copy, so you can pick exactly which copies go.

Open and close a group with the arrow next to it. Ticking a group ticks everything inside it, and a bar in the box means only some of it is ticked. To select every normal or shiny card, tick the Normal or Shiny box at the top of its column.

Two buttons at the top fill in the selection for you, each showing how much dust you would get:

* Prioritize Normal counts normal and shiny cards together and selects the extra shiny cards, along with any normal cards above the maximum
* Prioritize Shiny does the same the other way around, selecting the extra normal cards

Clear unticks everything. Look over the selection, change it by hand if you like, then press Disenchant. The button shows the dust you will get from what is ticked.

DETERMINATION cards and shiny BASE cards are never disenchanted.

It is turned off by default. To use it, turn off "Disable Smart Disenchanting" in the Crafting section of the Library settings.

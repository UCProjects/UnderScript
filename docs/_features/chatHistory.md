date: 2026-11-08
version: 0.66.0
---

Chat history survives page loads. The last 50 public messages of each room are still there when you go to another page, even after a break.

UnderScript keeps its own copy of the history and puts it back when a page loads.

Turn it off with "Disable keeping chat history between page loads" in the settings.

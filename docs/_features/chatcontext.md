A context menu is available to easily perform user-specific actions.

Clicking or right clicking a user in chat opens a profile card with their avatar and level. For Master and above it also shows their division, with their Elo over the icon, because the game does not share anyone's division below Master. Below it are the actions: Message and Mute for moderators only, Ignore, and a Spectate option for users who are in a game. Ignore and Mute are hidden, instead of greyed out, for yourself and for moderators.

The Note button keeps a note about a user on your computer. Hover it to read the note and click it to edit. Ctrl+Enter, or clicking away, saves, and Escape drops the change.

Holding Ctrl while right clicking still shows the browser's own menu, and Ctrl+click still opens the game's profile, which is also one option away in the card. "Disable Chat Context (right click)" turns the right click card off, and "Open the profile card on click" in the Chat settings turns the click card off.

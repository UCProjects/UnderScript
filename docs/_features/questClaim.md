---
title: Claim quests without reloading
date: 2026-11-08
version: 0.66.0
---

Claiming a quest no longer reloads the Quests page, the same way the Friendship page claims rewards. A toast shows the reward, and the Claim and replace-quest buttons are removed, like the game does.

If the claim fails, the page falls back to the normal form submit.

The Completed Quests block has a Claim All button.

Turn it off with "Disable Quest Claim Without Reload" in the settings.

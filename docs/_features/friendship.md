---
title: Friendship improvements
date: 2020-08-01
version: 0.39.0
---

* Friendship ranks for each of your cards
* A "Collect All" button to claim every available reward at once
* A toast when you receive a friendship reward
* A button on the leaderboard to rank only you and your friends
* The Friendship page's filters get the same dimmed icons as the Decks and Crafting pages, and "Trim filter buttons" set to Always hides their checkboxes too

Each of these can be turned off in the settings.
